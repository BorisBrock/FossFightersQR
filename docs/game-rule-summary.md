# Boss Fighters QR – Rule Summary

Base game plus the *Jungle Fever* expansion.

A **cooperative campaign card game for 2–4 players**. The Heroes fight a series of Bosses together. A **companion app** (smartphone or tablet) is required: it plays the Boss and reads your cards via the front camera (hold each card about 15 cm away). It also resolves card effects. Health is tracked on physical Health counters, not in the app.

## Goal

- **Win:** reduce the Boss's life to 0.
- **Lose:** **any single Hero** drops to 0 health.
- **Campaign:**
  - Base game: a training fight, then 10 Bosses.
  - Expansion: 5 more Bosses.
  - Elite Bosses can be fought afterwards.
- **Difficulty:** choose one of 4 levels before each fight.

## Setup (per player)

- Pick **1 Hero + 1 Class**.
- Shuffle their **12 Action cards** together as your face-down **draw pile**. Leave space for a **discard pile** and a **play area**.
- **Starting health** = Hero value + Class value.
- **Hand limit** = Hero value + Class value.
- Draw up to your hand limit.
- Scan your Hero and Class in the app and choose your seat.

## Round (7 phases)

1. **Planning:** the app shows each Hero's incoming **Boss attack** value, sometimes with an **additional effect** (e.g. Poison).
2. **Shield:** the Boss usually gains **3 shields**: Melee, Ranged and Magic.
3. **Action:**
   - Reveal any Curses first.
   - Each Hero normally has **3 Actions per round**.
   - Players do **not** take all 3 at once. They **alternate clockwise, one turn each**, with **1 Action per turn**, until everyone has had 3 turns. The group chooses who starts each round.
   - An Action is either *play 1 hand card* or *use 1 Item*. You scan the card or Item in the app.
   - **Extra Actions:** a **Lightning** symbol lets you take another Action right away, within the same turn. Other card effects and abilities can also add Actions (e.g. the Paladin's *Warhorse* gives a 4th Action in the next round).
4. **Attack:** each Hero takes damage equal to their remaining Boss attack, plus its additional effect.
5. **Status:** Poison and Fire take effect.
6. **Discard:** move played cards from your play area to your discard pile. Discard down to your hand limit, and you may discard extra cards too.
7. **Draw:** refill your hand up to your hand limit.

## Card effects

- **Attack** (Melee / Ranged / Magic): deals damage equal to its value.
  - Damage first breaks the Boss's **shield of that type**.
  - Only once that shield is gone does damage of that type reduce the Boss's life.
- **Support:** deals damage like an Attack, but **only if an Attack of the same type was already played this round** (by anyone). Supports are *not* Attacks.
- **Protection:** lowers the Boss attack against any one Hero.
  - A Boss attack reduced to **exactly 0** deals no damage **and** cancels its additional effect.
- **Lightning:** gives you an extra Action immediately.
- **Heal:** restores health, but never above starting health.
- **Draw:** draw cards. You may exceed your hand limit until Phase 6.

## Core rules

- **Talk** openly about your cards, but **never mention card numbers**.
- **Played cards** stay in your play area until Phase 6, so no card can be played twice in a round.
- **Empty draw pile:** immediately shuffle your discard pile into a new draw pile. Cards in the play area are not included.
- **Golden Boss Rule:** Boss abilities are deliberately left unexplained in the rules. Discover the Boss's patterns together, such as recurring abilities, reactions to your actions and health thresholds, and plan around them.
- **Tied Boss targets:** you choose which of the tied Heroes is affected.
- **Between fights:**
  - Reset health.
  - Remove all tokens.
  - Shuffle your whole deck.
  - You may change your Hero and Class.

## Status tokens

Tokens are placed on your Hero card and stay until the end of the fight unless an effect removes them.

- **Poison:** lose 1 health per token in Phase 5.
- **Panic:** you may only play hand cards at random. After each random card, discard 1 Panic token.
- **Paralysis:** your hand limit is reduced by 1 per token.
- **Fire:** in Phase 5, draw 1 card per token. For each card, either remove it for the rest of the fight, **or** discard it and lose 2 health.

## Items

Items are unlocked over the campaign.

- **Backpack:** draw 3 cards. Unlimited uses.
- **Healing Potion:** one Hero heals 8. You get 1 per Hero per fight.
- **Lucky Charm:** play the top card of your draw pile. Lightning effects on it are ignored.
- **Medical Bag:** remove 2 of your own tokens.
- **Waterskin:** heal 3.

## Campaign progression

- **Loot cards:**
  - Earned after each Boss.
  - At most **1 per star level** in your deck.
  - You can re-choose them before every fight.
- **Deck size:** you may remove cards before a fight, but every fight starts with **at least 20 cards**.
- **Level-ups:** Heroes and Classes gain unique abilities and more health over the campaign.
- **Curses:**
  - When you receive a Curse, draw it face-down without looking.
  - Reveal it at the start of the next Action phase.
  - Most are discarded after that Action phase.
  - Instant Curses resolve immediately. Permanent Curses last the whole fight.
- **Boss-specific mechanics** appear only for certain Bosses, for example Slime cards and a Lost pile, Vines, Spiderlings, or locations to move between.

## Expansion: new Heroes and Classes

These are also usable in the base game, but they are harder to play.

- **Goblin** (offensive Hero): builds a **Battle Robot** that gives +2 to all his Attacks.
- **Dryad** (flexible Hero): switches between three forms:
  - Bark: +1 Protection
  - Thorn: +1 to Attacks and Supports
  - Blossom: heals when damaging the Boss
- **Paladin** (defensive Class): protects and heals. Can gain a 4th Action in a round.
- **Shaman** (support Class): plays Totems that buff one Hero until the end of the round.
- **Pass card:** forfeits an Action. You must play it if no Action is possible.
